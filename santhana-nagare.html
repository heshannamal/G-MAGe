The story / Santhana Nagare

More than a performance.
A feeling.

Santhana Nagare — Live in Concert is an intimate musical journey inspired by the feeling of a city after dark: moonlit reflections, familiar melodies and stories carried through song. Conceived as an elegant celebration of Sri Lankan music, it blends expressive live vocals, instrumental arrangements and an immersive golden stage atmosphere.
